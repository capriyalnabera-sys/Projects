Methodology & sample sizes

How this works: Every batch refresh writes a snapshot of the verdict for each stock. For each historical snapshot, we then look up the actual price 5, 20, and 60 trading days later, and compute realized returns. Those returns get bucketed by what the verdict was on the snapshot date.

  • Big-win threshold: +{{ result.big_win_threshold_pct }}% (used in advanced table)

Reading the n=… values: these are sample sizes. n=5 means just 5 data points — wide uncertainty. n=100+ starts to be statistically meaningful. Sparse cells with — are insufficient data, not failed calls.
